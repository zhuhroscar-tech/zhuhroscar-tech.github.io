10.2Important notice

Hypothetical results. All performance before October 19, 2026 was calculated after the fact by applying the strategy's rules to historical prices. No trades were made and no money was at risk. Simulations benefit from hindsight in the choice of universe, signals and settings, and cannot fully reflect liquidity, market impact or order rejections.

10.3How each period was produced

PeriodMethod
Feb 2016 – Dec 2024Walk-forward: for each year Y the model was retrained with the original research code on 2010 to Y−2, validated on Y−1, and scored Y. Signals were executed at the next open in a share-and-cash simulator with 10 bps per trade, drifting weights and 0% on cash. The first 19 sessions of each year carry no new signal, a quirk of the original code, so those rebalances are skipped.
Jan 2, 2025 – Oct 16, 2026Frozen-model replay: the production model file and live code on dates after all of its training data, with next-open fills, whole shares and 10 bps per trade. First run Oct 19, 2026; not registered in advance.
From Oct 19, 2026Paper account: actual Alpaca paper fills and positions, read without placing orders.
ReferencesSOXX and SPY bought at the first open and held. Matched basket: all 30 stocks in equal weight, scaled daily by SOXR-10's exposure.

10.4Definitions

NAV per unitPortfolio value per unit; every series starts at $10.00.
Sharpe ratioAnnualized mean daily return ÷ annualized volatility, 0% risk-free rate.
Maximum drawdownLargest fall from a previous NAV peak.
BetaSensitivity of daily returns to SOXX.
Up / down captureAverage strategy return in months SOXX rose (fell), as a share of SOXX's average in those months.
ICRank correlation between model scores and the next 20 days' returns at each rebalance; t is its mean ÷ standard error.
