Erratum 2
FIXED

Original backtest credited same-day returns

The research simulator gave newly selected stocks the return of the day they were selected, and re-weighted daily without charging for it. Its claimed 2016–2024 result was Sharpe 1.02, +23.6% a year, −27.3% maximum drawdown.

Workaround / resolution: Rev B re-ran the period with next-open execution and share-level accounting: Sharpe , a year, maximum drawdown.

Erratum 3
OPEN

Signals and volatility settings chosen with test-period data

The 33 signals and the 22% / 20-day regulator settings were selected using data from 2016–2024, so that period is partly in-sample and flatters the result.

Workaround / resolution: Use the 2025–2026 replay and the paper record as the cleaner tests.

Erratum 4
OPEN

Live and training factors differ for 6 of 33 signals

On identical prices, 27 of 33 live signals match the training signals exactly. The six days-since-high/low signals (IMIN, IMAX) are offset by 1/window.

Workaround / resolution: Small effect; fix in the next model release.

Erratum 5
NOTE

Training data dated one trading day early

The local Qlib store used for training labels each bar one day early. Features, labels and prices shift together, so there is no look-ahead, but dates are mislabelled.

Workaround / resolution: Corrected before execution in the Rev B simulation; no action needed for live trading.

Erratum 6
CORRECTED

Model description in the original report

The report described a two-layer GRU with self-attention and two-phase training. The code uses one layer, additive attention and single-phase training.

Workaround / resolution: Section 7 describes the model as built.

Erratum 7
OPEN

Validation figures disagree

The project notes report a validation rank correlation of −0.061; the model file records +0.133, reached at the first training epoch. An optimum that early points to a weak signal.

Workaround / resolution: Re-measure validation when the model is next retrained.
