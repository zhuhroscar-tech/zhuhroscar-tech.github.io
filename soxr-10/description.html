7Detailed description

SOXR-10 converts daily market data for 30 semiconductor stocks into a ten-stock portfolio and an exposure level. Figure 7-1 shows the signal chain; each block is described in Section 7.2.

7.2Feature description

7.2.1Factor front end

On the rebalance morning, about 200 calendar days of daily open, high, low, close and volume are downloaded for each stock. 33 signals from Microsoft Qlib's Alpha158 library are computed: share of up and down days over 10–60 days, price–volume correlation, volume volatility, days since the recent high and low, and 10-day price quantiles. Each stock gets a 20-day history of these signals.

7.2.2GRU core and expert mux

A one-layer recurrent network (GRU, 32 units) with attention reads each stock's history. Three linear expert heads each forecast the next 20-day return, and a learned router selects one expert per stock. The production model was trained on 2010–2023 and selected on 2024; it is not retrained on a schedule.

7.2.3Top-10 select

The ten highest scores are held in equal dollar amounts. Any holding that drops out of the top ten is sold in full at the rebalance.

7.2.4Volatility regulator

Invested share = 22% ÷ the trailing 20-day annualized volatility of the equal-weight 30-stock basket, held between 20% and 100%. The remainder stays in cash and earns nothing.

7.2.5Order output and timing

Orders go to an Alpaca paper account as day market orders before the open. A scheduled job checks every weekday and trades once 20 weekdays have passed since the last rebalance.

7.3Functional modes

Normal: sector volatility below about 22%, so the portfolio is fully invested. Regulated: above that, the invested share falls in proportion, to about 60% at 37% volatility. Floor: above about 110% volatility the share stops at 20%; March 2020 came close, at about 22%.
