1Features

  • Holds 10 of the 30 Philadelphia Semiconductor Index constituents, re-selected every 20 trading days
  • Equal dollar weight per position; long only, no leverage
  • Volatility regulation: 22% annual target, 20–100% invested (Figure 4-3)
  • Ranks stocks from 33 price-and-volume signals with a recurrent neural network
  • Simulated Feb 2016 – Oct 2026, net of 10 bps per trade: a year, Sharpe
  • Paper-traded through Alpaca since October 19, 2026

2Applications

  • Semiconductor exposure with drawdown control
  • Research platform for machine-learning stock ranking
  • Benchmark for simpler volatility-targeted sector baskets
  • Not demonstrated: stock selection beyond the sector itself (Erratum 1)

3Description

SOXR-10 is a rules-based portfolio of US-listed semiconductor stocks. At each rebalance a neural network scores the 30 index members on recent price and volume behaviour; the ten highest scores are held in equal amounts.

A volatility regulator sets how much of the account is invested (Figure 4-3). Most of the simulated reduction in drawdowns comes from it.

The ranking has not yet shown an edge: holding all 30 stocks at the same exposure did about as well (Figure 4-4). Section 9, Errata lists this and every other known issue.

4Typical characteristics

Conditions: simulated, 10 bps per trade, next-open fills, every series rebased to $10. 2016–2024 walk-forward (model retrained yearly); shaded 2025–2026 frozen-model replay on prices it never saw.

5Device comparison

Matched basket: all 30 stocks in equal weight, scaled daily by SOXR-10's own exposure. It isolates what the ranking adds. Drawdown here covers the full period; Section 6 also gives 2016–2024 alone.
